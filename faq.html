COMMON QUESTIONS

General Questions

Here are some of the most common questions we get from our clients.

What is Nexora?

Nexora is a modern website template designed for businesses of all sizes. It helps you create a professional, high-converting website with a clean design, flexible components, and easy customization.

Who is Nexora for?

Nexora is designed for businesses, agencies, startups, freelancers, and professionals who need a modern website.

Do I need coding skills to use Nexora?

Basic HTML and CSS knowledge is helpful, but Nexora is structured to make customization as straightforward as possible.

Can I use Nexora for multiple projects?

You can use Nexora according to the license terms associated with your purchase.

Is Nexora SEO friendly?

Yes. Nexora uses clean HTML structure and semantic elements to provide a solid foundation for SEO optimization.

Do you provide updates?

Updates may include improvements, fixes, and compatibility enhancements depending on the product roadmap.

Where can I get support?

Support information and available support channels are provided with your Nexora purchase.
